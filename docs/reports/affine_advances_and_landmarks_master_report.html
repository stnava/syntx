5.1 Methodological Architecture of robust_affine(tournament=True)

The full tournament pipeline (_run_tournament_affine in src/syntx/robust_affine.py) resolves clinical convergence failures by pitting four distinct mathematical paradigms against each other under objective spatial gating:

1. Fast Lie Algebra Intensity Solver

Multi-start center-of-mass, moments-of-inertia, and directional cones optimized via analytical Mattes MI in the SE(3) Lie algebra.

2. Sampled Sinkhorn Optimal Transport

Random Point Selection + Sinkhorn OT: Subsamples 1–2% of foreground domain (500–8,000 points), extracts 12-channel physical MIND-SSC descriptors, and computes a continuous soft correspondence transport plan with dustbin outlier rejection + closed-form weighted Procrustes (no RANSAC needed).

3. SIFT3D Extrema + RANSAC

Physical millimeter scale-space DoG keypoints with 3D Hessian edge rejection, mutual Lowe-ratio matching (0.92), and Kabsch SVD / regularized affine RANSAC.

4. SO(3) Wide Rotation Search

Tests discrete 90°, 45°, and 180° pitch, roll, and yaw candidate rotations scored against identity via sampled MIND structural correlation.

Objective Selection & Fail-Safe Gating:
  1. Consensus Landmark TRE Gating: Mean Target Registration Error is scored against verified landmark correspondences. Any candidate with $\text{TRE} > 15\text{ mm}$ is automatically disqualified if any competitor achieved $\text{TRE} < 6\text{ mm}$.
  2. Salient Structural Correlation: Warps the moving volume by surviving candidates and scores gradient correlation across sharp tissue boundaries.
  3. Guaranteed Candidate Retention: Prevents unmasked thoracic or abdominal background air/fat at coarse resolution (Level 4) from prematurely pruning valid landmark candidates.
  4. Multi-Resolution Polish: Winning landmark/OT candidates undergo a final multi-resolution gradient polish to lock into sub-voxel anatomical boundaries.

5.1.1 Deep Dive: Continuous Sampled Sinkhorn Optimal Transport & Random Point Selection

Rather than relying solely on discrete extrema keypoints (SIFT3D) that require combinatorial RANSAC, syntx.landmarks.sampled_optimal_transport_affine introduces a continuous, percentage-sampled Optimal Transport approach:

  • Percentage-Based Random Sampling: Uniformly draws a deterministic percentage (1–2%, 500–8,000 points) of the anatomical foreground domain, guaranteeing scale-invariance and sub-second execution on GPU/MPS.
  • Physical 12-Channel MIND-SSC Descriptors: Self-similarity context computed directly in physical millimetres, ensuring complete invariance to intensity non-uniformity and modality differences.
  • Log-Space Sinkhorn with Dustbin: Solves entropy-regularized transport with a slack dustbin dimension (cost = 1.0) that absorbs outliers, occlusions, and non-overlapping anatomy without combinatorial search.
  • Closed-Form Weighted Procrustes: Computes the exact SVD rigid/affine matrix over the continuous soft transport plan, requiring zero RANSAC iterations.
