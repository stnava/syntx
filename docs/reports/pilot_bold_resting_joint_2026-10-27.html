Pilot: one joint objective for resting BOLD motion (breacher sub-6223, sub-3315)

Script: benchmarks/pilot_bold_resting_joint.py. One L-BFGS objective over all frames from a zero pose: Huber residual of the warped series about its robust temporal mean at a common gradient-weighted point set, per-point residual scale, a per-slice-group phase-encode field shift with a shrinkage prior, and a Charbonnier prior on the second differences of the rigid pose. Baseline: syntx.motion_correction(preset="bold"). Ablations: rigid_only (no field term), uniform_scale (no per-point scale). FD is Power FD at 50 mm; "FD notch" removes the near-Nyquist alternation band from the poses before FD. tsd_edge / tsd_fg: median relative temporal SD of the corrected series at tissue edges (top 20 % gradient) and in the foreground; lower is better alignment. dvars~fd: correlation of DVARS with FD. Caveat: tsd is close to the pilot's own criterion, so the alignment comparison favours the pilot; an independent downstream criterion is still needed.

Reading

  • The joint objective aligns the images slightly better than the baseline (tsd_edge 0.0323 vs 0.0340; 0.0515 vs 0.0524) while reporting more FD, not less: a more precise fit finds more of the alternation.
  • Notching the alternation out of the rigid poses before resampling makes the edges worse in both subjects and collapses the DVARS to FD correlation. The frame-to-frame alternation is a real displacement of the image content, whatever its physiological origin.
  • The field term takes 0.04 to 0.06 mm, 80 to 94 % of it inside the alternation band, with a 0.03 mm spread across slice groups; dropping it changes alignment by less than 1 %. The per-point scale changes nothing measurable.
  • For censoring and QC the inflation is a property of FD, not of the estimator: FD with the alternation band removed is 0.14 mm (6223) and 0.19 to 0.28 mm (3315) under every estimator.
