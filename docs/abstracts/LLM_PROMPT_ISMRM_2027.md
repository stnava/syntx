# Prompt for an LLM working on the ISMRM 2027 motion-solver abstract

You are editing a conference abstract for Brian Avants (University of Virginia) about a motion solver for MRI
time series in the open-source `syntx` library. Your output is the abstract text and figure captions. You do not

## The claim, in one paragraph

One optimisation registers every frame of an MRI time series against the mean of its own acquisition level
(b-shell, ASL label state, flow-encoding state) with the acquisition design inside the objective. Terms that physics
calls displacements (the eddy-current affine along phase encode, its Jacobian) are applied to the data; terms that
physics calls contrast or design-locked pose offsets are removed from the reported motion and never applied. The
head trajectory carries a prior on its second differences whose form is re-descending (Cauchy): the pull peaks at a
knee of 0.05 mm and fades for larger steps, so drift and real motion pass through. The prior's weight (0.03 in
curvature units for multi-level designs, 0.05 for one-level designs such as BOLD, since syntx 6.29.0) was chosen by a gate on three phantoms with known motion and five clinical series. The result
reports, per acquisition axis and per voxel, the share of variance held by head motion, by applied acquisition
terms and by removed design-locked terms.

## Hard constraints

- ISMRM format: synopsis of at most 100 words; body of at most 850 words (Introduction, Methods, Results,
  Discussion, Conclusion); up to five figures with captions that stand alone; references as given.
- Every quantitative claim traces to one of the files above. If you cannot find a number, write "not yet measured";
  never estimate.
- The data term of the default engine is Mattes mutual information against the frame's level reference. It is NOT
  temporal variance and NOT a Huber residual; do not describe it as either.
- The design enters the default engine in two places only: the per-level references (contrast) and the pose-domain
  columns $D_k\beta$ (design-locked offsets removed from the reported pose). Do not say the design is "projected out
  of the metric"; that is a separate, non-default engine.
- The chain written per frame is `[rigid, eddy]`; a transform list acts on the reference-space point first-listed
  first, so this reproduces the fitted eddy(rigid(x)).
- FD is computed from the head pose $m_k$ only. The band-limited FD is for censoring only; the full poses are the
  correction.
- The prior's weight is relative to the measured curvature of the image term ("curvature units"); say so once.
- The hypothesis that small second differences should be shrunk hardest was REFUTED on the phantoms. State it as a
  negative result with its numbers; do not soften it and do not omit it.
- Known limitations must appear in the Discussion: the phantoms plant jitter in the trajectory, not in the images
  (so "sub-knee jitter is measurement noise" has no phantom gate yet); the 60-volume ASL image-domain account is not
  interpreted; censoring thresholds are not tied to a downstream outcome; the design-in-metric engine over-reports
  readout jitter on real DWI and is not default.
- Software versions: syntx 6.29.0 (6.28.0 set the 0.03 default; 6.29.0 added 0.05 for one-level designs), antsxdwi 0.33.x, antsxfunctional 0.23.x, antsxslowflow 2.12.x. Earlier versions
  (6.24 to 6.26) had a Newton unit bug and a chain-order bug; numbers from them are superseded and must not appear.
